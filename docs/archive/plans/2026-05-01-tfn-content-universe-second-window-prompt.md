# TFN Content Universe Second-Window Prompt

Use this prompt in a separate Codex/AI window while the main thread continues polishing and deploying the TFN V2 product shell.

---

You are helping me develop the Tooth Fairy Network content universe.

Project context:

Tooth Fairy Network is a product for parents and children around ages 5-10. A child loses a tooth, a parent helps them mint the memory as a digital keepsake, and family can contribute to a parent-controlled Smile Fund. The default suggested unlock age is 10. The product is simple: a keepsake, a family gift link, and a small savings account. The story universe is what makes the product delightful, shareable, and emotionally memorable.

The main character is Tanda, the origin tooth fairy and emerging leader of the Tooth Fairy Network. The folklore premise is that tooth traditions around the world are not separate accidents. They are local branches of a hidden global network: fairies, mice, birds, sun spirits, family rituals, and other collectors all helping children mark the moment when a tooth is lost. In the modern version, Tanda is connecting these traditions into a magical network that maps loosely onto blockchain rails: teeth become memories, memories become keepsakes, and family gifts become the child&apos;s first lesson in saving.

What I want from this working session:

1. Build the content bible for the Tooth Fairy Network.
2. Define Tanda&apos;s role, personality, visual consistency, powers, limitations, and emotional arc.
3. Define the global network: its stations, rules, collectors, rituals, conflicts, and visual motifs.
4. Define the first 10-15 public stories that should launch with the site.
5. Create a repeatable story brief template so we can expand from 10 stories to 50-100 stories without losing quality.
6. Develop animation concepts that visually explain the product without overexplaining it in text.
7. Keep the product goal in mind: parents should understand the product, children should want the stories, and families should want to share culturally specific tales.

Important creative direction:

- The stories must be entertaining for children, not just educational.
- Parents should enjoy reading them at bedtime.
- Each story should have a simple conflict, humor, warmth, and a satisfying emotional turn.
- Each culture&apos;s tradition should be treated respectfully and researched before being fictionalized.
- The blockchain metaphor should stay magical and subtle. Do not make children read about technical architecture.
- Tanda should connect traditions, not erase them.
- The villain/conflict layer can include a Babylonian tooth-worm figure, but do not make the universe too dark or scary for 5-10-year-olds.
- The stories should support the product idea: little things can become meaningful when they are saved, remembered, and allowed to grow.

Start by producing:

1. A concise content-universe thesis.
2. A character bible for Tanda.
3. A map of the Tooth Fairy Network as a magical system.
4. A proposed first-season story list of 10-15 stories, including region, tradition, central character, conflict, lesson, and visual asset needs.
5. Three animation concepts for the homepage:
   - a 10-second landing animation,
   - a 30-second product explainer,
   - a 60-90 second lore trailer.
6. A reusable story brief template.
7. A list of open questions for Sathian before story generation begins.

Do not write final full stories yet. First help structure the universe, identify the best launch slate, and define what "good" looks like.
